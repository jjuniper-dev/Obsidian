# ISO/IEC 22989 — AI System Concepts

*Source: ISO/IEC 22989:2022 — Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Concepts and terminology*

## Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Property of a system that enables it to acquire, process, and apply knowledge and skills—including reasoning, learning, perception, and language understanding—in ways that would typically require human intelligence.

**Related:** AI system, Machine learning, Algorithm

---

## AI System

An engineered system that generates outputs such as content, forecasts, recommendations, or decisions for a given set of objectives. An AI system interacts with the environment, processing inputs to produce outputs using AI models.

**Related:** AI model, Inference, Deployment

---

## AI Model

A mathematical construct or set of algorithms trained on data to recognize patterns, make predictions, or generate outputs. The model encodes the learned representation of the training data.

**Related:** Training data, Machine learning, Inference

---

## Algorithm

A finite set of well-defined rules or instructions that a computer can execute to solve a problem or accomplish a task, potentially including learning from data.

**Related:** AI model, Machine learning

---

## Machine Learning (ML)

A type of artificial intelligence in which a system acquires capabilities through exposure to data rather than through explicit programming. The model improves its performance on a task through experience.

**Related:** Deep learning, Training data, Supervised learning

---

## Deep Learning

A subfield of machine learning based on artificial neural networks with multiple layers (deep neural networks), capable of learning hierarchical representations from raw data.

**Related:** Neural network, Machine learning, Foundation model

---

## Neural Network

A computational model loosely inspired by the structure of biological neural networks, comprising interconnected nodes (neurons) organized in layers that process and transform input signals to produce outputs.

**Related:** Deep learning, Machine learning, AI model

---

## Foundation Model

A large AI model trained on broad, general data at scale that can be adapted to a wide range of downstream tasks through fine-tuning or prompting. Includes large language models (LLMs) and large multimodal models.

**Related:** Large language model, Fine-tuning, Transfer learning

---

## Large Language Model (LLM)

A foundation model trained on large corpora of text data that learns statistical relationships between tokens, enabling it to generate, summarize, translate, and reason about natural language.

**Related:** Foundation model, Generative AI, Inference

---

## Generative AI

AI systems capable of generating new content—such as text, images, audio, code, or synthetic data—that resembles but is not copied from training data.

**Related:** Foundation model, Large language model, Synthetic data
