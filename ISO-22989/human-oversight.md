# ISO/IEC 22989 — Human Oversight

*Source: ISO/IEC 22989:2022 — Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Concepts and terminology*

## Human-in-the-Loop

An operational mode in which a human is actively involved in each decision cycle of an AI system—reviewing, approving, or overriding AI outputs before they take effect.

**Related:** Human-on-the-loop, Accountability, AI system

---

## Human-on-the-Loop

An operational mode in which AI system outputs are executed automatically but a human monitors the system and retains the ability to intervene or override within a defined time window.

**Related:** Human-in-the-loop, Monitoring, Accountability

---

## Human-over-the-Loop

An operational mode in which an AI system operates fully autonomously at the execution level, with human oversight limited to governance-level review of objectives, policies, and system performance over time.

**Related:** Human-on-the-loop, Accountability, Transparency

---

## Operator

A natural or legal person that deploys or uses an AI system within a product or service context, responsible for its appropriate configuration and use within the bounds set by the developer.

**Related:** Developer, User, Accountability

---

## Affected Party

An individual or group who experiences outcomes or impacts from an AI system's decisions or recommendations, whether or not they are the direct user of the system.

**Related:** Fairness, Accountability, User
