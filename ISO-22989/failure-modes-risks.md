# ISO/IEC 22989 — Failure Modes & Risks

*Source: ISO/IEC 22989:2022 — Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Concepts and terminology*

## Hallucination

A phenomenon in generative AI systems in which the model produces outputs that are factually incorrect, fabricated, or inconsistent with the input, while appearing plausible or confident.

**Related:** Generative AI, Reliability, Explainability

---

## Adversarial Attack

A deliberate perturbation of inputs—often imperceptible to humans—designed to cause an AI model to produce incorrect outputs, misclassifications, or security breaches.

**Related:** Security, Robustness, Safety

---

## Data Poisoning

An attack in which an adversary corrupts training data with maliciously crafted examples to cause a trained model to behave incorrectly or in a targeted manner at inference time.

**Related:** Adversarial attack, Security, Training data

---

## Distribution Shift

A change in the statistical properties of input data encountered during deployment compared to training, potentially causing model performance to degrade in ways not anticipated during development.

**Related:** Robustness, Monitoring, Overfitting

---

## Uncertainty Quantification

Methods for estimating and communicating the confidence or reliability of AI model outputs, enabling downstream decision-makers to understand when predictions are likely to be unreliable.

**Related:** Reliability, Explainability, Model evaluation
