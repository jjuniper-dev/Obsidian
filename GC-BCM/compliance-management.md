# 5.0 — Compliance Management

Enforcement, oversight, and verification adhering to regulatory requirements.

**Domain:** Core Mandate / Policy

## Sub-capabilities

### 5.1 — Oversight, Monitoring & Audit

Conducting ongoing oversight and formal internal/external audits to verify compliance with laws or policies.

### 5.2 — Inspection & Enforcement Actions

Executing physical or digital inspections and issuing sanctions, fines, or directives for non-compliance.

### 5.3 — Investigations & Evidence Management

Conducting detailed investigations into suspected regulatory or criminal infractions and preserving evidence.
