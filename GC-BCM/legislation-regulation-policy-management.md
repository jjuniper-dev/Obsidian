# 1.0 — Legislation, Regulation & Policy Management

Formulating, developing, evaluating, and operationalizing federal legal frameworks and directive instruments.

**Domain:** Core Mandate / Policy

## Sub-capabilities

### 1.1 — Policy Development & Alignment

Researching, analyzing, and developing policy options and instruments aligned with government priorities.

### 1.2 — Legislative Drafting & Support

Drafting bills, regulations, and orders-in-council, and providing legislative process support.

### 1.3 — Regulatory Impact Assessment & Governance

Evaluating the socio-economic and environmental impacts of regulations and managing regulatory frameworks.
