# 9.0 — Corporate Management

Internal administration, corporate legal services, and shared horizontal enablement.

**Domain:** Enabling & Corporate Support

## Sub-capabilities

### 9.1 — Corporate Governance & Executive Support

Providing secretariat support to executive committees, ministerial correspondence, and administrative oversight.

### 9.2 — Corporate Legal Services

Providing internal legal counsel, advisory services, and litigation support to the department or agency.

### 9.3 — Security, Continuity & Emergency Management

Managing personnel security screening, physical security, business continuity planning (BCP), and occupational health.
