# 6.0 — Program & Service Delivery

Front-office capabilities delivering external public-facing programs (grants, contributions, licensing, benefits).

**Domain:** Operations & Delivery

## Sub-capabilities

### 6.1 — Service Design & Experience Management

Designing user-centric public services and managing multi-channel delivery (web, in-person, phone).

### 6.2 — Grants & Contributions Management

Administering, tracking, and disbursing transfer payments, grants, and contributions to external recipients.

### 6.3 — Benefits Delivery & Direct Assistance

Processing applications, calculating eligibility, and distributing direct benefit payments to individuals or businesses.

### 6.4 — Licensing, Permitting & Registration

Issuing, renewing, suspending, or tracking official licenses, permits, certificates, and registries.
