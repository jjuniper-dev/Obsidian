# 4.0 — Relationship Management

Managing citizen, stakeholder, G2G (government-to-government), and vendor interactions.

**Domain:** Operations & Delivery

## Sub-capabilities

### 4.1 — Citizen & Stakeholder Engagement

Consulting and collaborating with Canadians, civil society, and stakeholders to inform decision-making.

### 4.2 — Public Communications & Media Relations

Managing official communications, public announcements, and interactions with the media.

### 4.3 — Intergovernmental & International Relations

Managing relationships, agreements, and collaborations with provincial, territorial, Indigenous, or international governments.
