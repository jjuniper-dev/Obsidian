# 7.0 — Information Management

Data/information lifecycle management, registry management, and corporate knowledge networks.

**Domain:** Enabling & Corporate Support

## Sub-capabilities

### 7.1 — Data Governance, Analytics & Operations

Managing data assets, data quality, open data compliance, and advanced analytics architectures.

### 7.2 — Records & Document Lifecycle Management

Capturing, storing, classifying, retaining, and disposing of information resources and physical/digital records.

### 7.3 — Access to Information & Privacy (ATIP)

Processing statutory requests for information and managing privacy impact assessments (PIAs).
