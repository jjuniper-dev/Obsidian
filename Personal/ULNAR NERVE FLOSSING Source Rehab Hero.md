ULNAR NERVE FLOSSING
[Source: Rehab Hero](https://share.google/0KMVL39ZFzYroDNdB)

The webpage outlines an Ulnar Nerve Flossing exercise protocol designed by Rehab Hero.

## Core Purpose

- Reduces nerve pain and localized inflammation.
- Mobilizes tissue without causing active nerve tension or stretching.

## Step-by-Step Execution Instructions

1. Tilt your head toward the active arm.
2. Simultaneously extend your wrist and bend your elbow.
3. Tilt your head away from the active arm.
4. Simultaneously straighten your arm and rotate your wrist.
