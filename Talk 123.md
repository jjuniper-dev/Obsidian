- OpenAI announced **GPT Live** (powered by GPT Live 1), a new voice model rolling out in ChatGPT, designed for more natural interaction with computers, phones, and other devices.
- Current voice models are limited by unnatural turn-taking, difficulty handling interruptions, and poor performance in noisy environments—problems GPT Live aims to solve.
![[Recording 20260711144910.m4a]]

- The model is **full duplex**, meaning it can listen and speak simultaneously (like a real phone conversation), continuously processing input and output at the same time with no rigid turns.
- It uses **delegation for deeper work**: GPT Live handles conversation while delegating complex questions to a more powerful model (GPT 5.5) in parallel, bridging the intelligence gap with text models.
- A demo showed **live language coaching**, where the model proactively interrupted an English learner to gently correct grammar and word choice in real time.
- A demo showed **real-time bidirectional translation** (English–Chinese and English–French), pausing intelligently to translate for meaning and flow rather than word-for-word.
- A demo showed **integrated web search and reasoning**, with the model naturally fact-checking dates, checking transit delays and weather, and pulling up sports scores and schedules mid-conversation.
- The model includes an **intelligence picker**, letting users choose between a fast "instant" model or higher reasoning levels for complex queries.
- Natural conversational elements like backchannels, laughter, and quick back-and-forth exchanges make the model feel more human and easier to talk to.
- **Safety was prioritized** from the start of training, with safeguards such as steering away from risky conversations.
- Presenters framed GPT Live as a step toward accessible AGI, where talking to AI feels like a real conversation; it is available in ChatGPT starting the day of the announcement.
