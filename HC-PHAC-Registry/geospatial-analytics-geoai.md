# Geospatial Analytics (GeoAI)

Geo-spatial analytics capability enabling climate and environmental monitoring, including analysis of air quality, extreme weather events, environmental exposures and geographic risk factors. Supports climate-health surveillance, vulnerability mapping and evidence-based public health and regulatory decision-making through integration of satellite imagery, sensor data and geographic information systems (GIS).
